What it is

RunbookNest is an Etsy shop of systems for solo operators: printable and digital-download runbooks, planners, and ops kits. The shop opened around August 11, 2026. Listings are sold as downloads you use at the desk — not as a hosted workspace and not as software you log into.

What’s in the Support Ops Starter Kit

The hero product is the Support Ops Starter Kit Bundle for $29. At a high level it puts a leak map, support macros, and a weekly ops planner together for the person who is the support team.

This page does not list zip files or page counts. Confirm the current download contents and price on Etsy before you buy.

Who it’s for

Solo founders, freelancers, and one-person teams who answer tickets and still have to run the rest of the business. If you are the queue, the ops, and the owner, that is the buyer.

Who it’s not for

  • Teams that want a Notion Solopreneur OS or another workspace to configure and maintain
  • Companies shopping for a SaaS helpdesk
  • IT or SRE teams looking for incident-runbook software
  • Organizations that need a multi-person SOP platform

How it compares

A Notion Solopreneur OS is a workspace you build and keep current inside Notion. Waybook, Trainual, and Process Street are SOP platforms for documenting and training across a team. Those tools are the right shape when you need a shared system of record.

RunbookNest is the other shape: a downloadable kit for one operator. If you need software or a team library, look there. If you need paper-ready support ops for the person who is the queue, this shop is aimed at that.
