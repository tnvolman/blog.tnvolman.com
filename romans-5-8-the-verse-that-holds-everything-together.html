Romans 5:8 — The Verse That Holds Everything Together

I didn't deserve to be found. The trajectory of my life should have gone a different direction. But while I was still hiding on Sunday mornings, love kept showing up anyway.

Michael McClanahan · August 12, 2026 · 4 min read

That verse has followed me for decades. Not as a slogan. As the only honest explanation for my life.

"But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us."
— Romans 5:8 (KJV)

I know what it looks like when love waits for you to get your act together first. That kind of love never arrives. Romans 5:8 doesn't wait. It moves toward people who are still hiding — shy, inconsistent, half-in, half-out, unsure they even want to be found.

I was that kid. Some Sundays I got on the bus. Some Sundays I stayed under the covers. God didn't wait for a perfect attendance record. He sent people who kept coming back, and through them He kept putting the gospel in front of me until I finally trusted Christ as my Savior at thirteen.

I didn't clean myself up and then get loved. I was loved, and then everything changed.

"While we were yet sinners — not after we improved, not after we proved we were worth the trip — Christ died for us."

That's the order that still wrecks me. While we were yet sinners — not after we improved, not after we proved we were worth the trip — Christ died for us. The cross wasn't a reward for the religious. It was rescue for people whose lives were already pointed the wrong way.

Looking back, I can see how close I was to a totally different story. Divorce. Distance from church. A home without the gospel as the center. There's no natural reason I end up a believer, a husband to Tonia, a dad to godly kids, and now a builder trying to serve the Church. The only reason that holds is Romans 5:8.

So when I talk about The Forge — about technology genuinely grounded in Scripture, not just decorated with a verse — this is what I mean. The tools can change. The mission can't: show up for people who don't have it all together yet. Keep coming back. Tell the truth about grace. Trust God with the rest.

If your story feels like it should have gone another direction, you're not disqualified. You might be exactly where Romans 5:8 has always done its best work.

"While we were yet sinners. That's not the footnote. That's the whole point."
